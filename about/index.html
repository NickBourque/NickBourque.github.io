I like owning a system end to end: the schema, the API, the deploy, and the dashboard that tells you it's working. I build only what's needed, but leave room for the next ten times the load.

Right now that means enterprise AR/VR device management at ArborXR: public APIs other companies build on, audit trails people can trust, and getting content onto headsets quickly. Before that I built backends for localized edtech and B2B SaaS for professional associations.

I approach problems pragmatically, lean on proven design patterns, and stay open to learning something new.
